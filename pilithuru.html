Who We Are

Sri Lanka's Finest,
Delivered Worldwide

"Our mission is to deliver premium, export-quality products to global markets while supporting local agriculture and promoting Sri Lanka's rich natural heritage."

Pilithuru Pvt Ltd is a Sri Lankan-based company specializing in high-quality dehydrated food products, herbal ingredients, spices, and handcrafted teas.

We work closely with local farmers and suppliers to source the finest raw materials, ensuring sustainability and freshness at every step. Using modern dehydration techniques, we preserve the natural essence, nutrients, and aroma of each product.
